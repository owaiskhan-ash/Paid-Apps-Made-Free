STRAIGHT ANSWERS

App deals, explained

Clear answers about temporary free promotions, store prices, and what to check before installing.

What does “paid apps made free” mean?

It describes an app or game that normally has an upfront price but is temporarily offered at zero cost by its developer or app store. A zero price can change without notice and may vary by country.

Are paid apps that are free today free forever?

Not necessarily. A promotion may end at any time. Check the current price on the official app-store listing before you install; availability and price can differ by account or region.

Does a free app include all features?

No. A free download can still contain ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid features. Review the store’s purchase details and the developer’s terms before installing.

How can I check if an app deal is legitimate?

Open the app’s official Google Play or Apple App Store listing, confirm the app and developer, and check that the displayed price is zero for your region. Avoid APK mirrors and never treat a screenshot or countdown as proof.
