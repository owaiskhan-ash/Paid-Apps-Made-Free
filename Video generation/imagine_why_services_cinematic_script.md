## Core message

Imagine removes the usual blockers between an idea and a launched product: technical complexity, upfront development cost, and ongoing infrastructure worry. You describe the idea; Imagine shapes, designs, builds, hosts and maintains it under a simple running plan.

- Zero build cost / no app development cost.
- ₹2,500 per month, billed quarterly at ₹7,500.
- Yearly plan: ₹25,000 instead of ₹30,000 — save ₹5,000.
- Domain, hosting and maintenance included.
- No technical knowledge needed.
- Inquiry flow starts on WhatsApp.
